Common Small Words

The

Used to point to a specific item

Example: The book is on the table.

And

Used to connect words or groups of words

Example: I like apples and oranges.

To

Indicates direction or purpose

Example: I went to school.

A/An

Used with singular nouns

Example: I saw a bird. I ate an apple.

Is

Form of the verb "to be"

Example: She is happy.
